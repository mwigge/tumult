Approval chain (change management)

Approval-gated runs (tiers T1–T3) execute only after explicit human approval — the change-management evidence for SOC 2 CC8.1. Pin hashes are re-verified at dispatch, and the full per-run, hash-chained `run_audit` trail is available via the API.
