Approval chain (change management)

Approval-gated runs (tiers T1–T3) execute only after explicit human approval — the change-management evidence for SOC 2 CC8.1. Each request pins the exact change content by hash, enforces an approver quorum with segregation of duties, and lapses on a TTL; approvals are single-use and break-glass overrides carry a mandatory justification. Pin hashes are re-verified at dispatch, and the full per-run, hash-chained `run_audit` trail is available via the API. Runs are shown by their id prefix; the full id and pin hash are available via the run-detail API.
